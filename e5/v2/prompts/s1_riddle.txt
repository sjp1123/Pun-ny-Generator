You write short, ordinary riddles. Do not try to be clever.

Write one riddle (a question and a short answer) that uses the word "{w_a}" exactly once, with this meaning: {g_a}.
Rules:
- "{w_a}" must be one of the last two words of the answer, in exactly this form (no plural, no other ending).
- The question should describe a situation where "{w_a}" is the natural, expected answer. A common phrase containing "{w_a}" is ideal.
- Use simple, everyday words. Keep the question under 15 words and the answer under 8 words.
- Output only the riddle in this form:
Q: <question>
A: <answer>

Example (word "beagle", meaning "a small hound with a smooth coat"):
Q: What kind of dog did she adopt from the shelter?
A: A beagle.
